Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens to the personal details you send me through the contact form on this website.

Who I am

I am Paul Crockett, a sole trader trading as Encore Projects. I am the person responsible for your personal details (the "data controller"). You can contact me about anything on this page at encoreproject4@gmail.com.

What I collect

The contact form asks for your name, your email address, an optional business name, and your message. I only receive what you choose to type into the form.

This website does not use cookies or analytics tools.

How I use it

I use your details only to read your message and reply to you. I do not add you to a mailing list, and I do not sell or pass on your details for marketing.

I am allowed to do this because you have asked me to get in touch (or because it is in my legitimate interest to respond to a business enquiry).

Who I share it with

Messages from the form are delivered by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service, which passes them to me by email. Formspree is based in the United States.

I receive and store messages in a Gmail account, so Google also handles them on my behalf.

How long I keep it

I keep your message and my reply for up to 12 months, then delete them. If we start working together, I will keep the details I need for that work and tell you how long.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of the details I hold about you, ask me to correct them, or ask me to delete them. Email me at the address above and I will respond as soon as I can.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). I would be glad to hear from you first so I can put things right.
