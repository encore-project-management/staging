About me

How I can help

You do not need a fully defined project to start a conversation. You may have an opportunity you want to explore, a change you need to make, or a sense that the business could work better.

  • Find clarity and set priorities

    When there are more ideas than hours in the day, I can help you step back, work through the options and decide what deserves attention first.

  • Plan and manage change

    If you are introducing a new way of working, I can help you turn the idea into a manageable plan, with clear responsibilities and practical next steps.

  • Improve the way things work

    If everyday tasks feel harder than they should, I can help you look at the process, understand what is getting in the way and identify sensible improvements.

  • Bring people with you

    Change depends on people understanding what is happening and why. I can help you think through communication, involve your team and work through concerns.

The starting point is your business and what you want to achieve. We can discuss where my experience may be useful and what an appropriate piece of work could involve.

My approach

I believe useful support starts with listening and leads to action you can realistically take.

  1. Understand your business

    I start by listening: what you want to achieve, what is working well and where you need help. Your priorities shape the conversation.

  2. Agree a useful focus

    Together, we identify where support could make a difference and agree a clear scope, priorities and what success would look like.

  3. Turn ideas into action

    I help break the work into practical steps, with clear ownership and a pace that fits the demands of your business.

  4. Review and adjust

    We look at what is changing, what you are learning and what needs to happen next. The aim is progress that your business can carry forward.

My aim is to keep things clear, proportionate and useful. The approach should fit your business and the people doing the work.
